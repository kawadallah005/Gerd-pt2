# STEP 1: Training — What Is and Isn't a Mass Desire

**1. In 3–4 sentences: why is a mass desire vague, what does "desire comes from lack / gain pleasure or avoid pain" mean, and why is fulfillment-scene verbatim usually thin (and what do you do about it)?**

A mass desire has to stay vague because vagueness is what lets millions of different people with millions of different backstories all recognize themselves in it — the instant you add specifics, you've written for one person, not a market. "Desire comes from lack" means the feeling exists because something wanted is missing, and that lack always shows up as one of two mirrored pulls: moving toward a wanted state (gain pleasure) or moving away from an unwanted one (avoid pain) — same root desire, two faces. Fulfillment-scene verbatim is thin because the research pool is made of people still living in the problem; nobody being interviewed in their pain has lived her future yet, so there's no quote of her there. The fix isn't to flag a gap — it's to script the fulfillment scene myself, built from the real, concrete details of her documented present life but authored forward by me as the "chronicler of her future."

**2. Three original e-com vague desires, each with its pleasure pole + pain pole + LF8 root, then one lack scene and one fulfillment scene for one of them:**

- **Weighted blanket.** Pleasure pole: to feel calm / held. Pain pole: to stop feeling wired and alone at night. LF3 (freedom from fear/pain) with a thread of LF7 (care — the blanket standing in for being cared for).
- **LED red-light therapy mask.** Pleasure pole: to feel radiant. Pain pole: to stop feeling worn out and aged. LF8 (social approval).
- **Posture corrector.** Pleasure pole: to feel confident / commanding. Pain pole: to stop feeling small or apologetic. LF6 (to be superior, keeping up with others).

For the weighted blanket:
- *Lack scene (would be in research):* she scrolls her phone at 1 a.m. with every light in the apartment off except the screen, too wired to try closing her eyes again.
- *Fulfillment scene (scripted from her life):* same apartment, same hour, but the phone's face-down on the nightstand and she's already asleep on her side before the show she put on even finishes.

**3. Classify:**

- "I want my hair back" — **DEMAND** (product result; ladders down to feel attractive / stop feeling invisible, LF8).
- "I want to feel attractive" — **DESIRE**. Pleasure pole: feel attractive. Pain pole: stop feeling invisible/unwanted. LF8 (social approval).
- "I want to stop being scared this is permanent" — **SITUATIONAL** ("permanent" smuggles the backstory; strip to feel safe / stop feeling afraid, LF3).
- "I want to keep the weight I lost" — **QUALIFIER** (only true because she's already in-market post-loss).
- "I don't think these vitamins work" — **BELIEF** ("I think that generic supplements won't fix this").
- "I want to feel beautiful in the mirror" — **DESIRE**. Pleasure pole: feel beautiful. Pain pole: stop feeling ugly/ashamed of her reflection. LF8 (social approval).

Ready for Step 2 — send the research.
